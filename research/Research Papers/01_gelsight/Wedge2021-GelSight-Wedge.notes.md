# Wedge2021 - notes
Claim: compact finger keeps 3D recon with careful optics; extends to 1-2 lights.
Method: ball cal 2.4mm + Hough + px/mm, RGBXY->GxGy map, curved gel = uniform light.
Numbers: $16 cam, 60FPS 640x480, 30 cal images enough (vs 1000s for NN).
Argus: use on curved probe; fallback 2-light mode when 3 LEDs don't fit.
